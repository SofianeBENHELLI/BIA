# Visual resources and interactive tools for BIA teaching (license-checked catalogue)

Method note: Wikimedia Commons licenses below were read directly from the Commons API (`extmetadata.LicenseShortName` + `Artist`) on 2026-11-22. File page URL pattern: `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:<name>` (spaces -> underscores). Commons API rate-limited (HTTP 429) part of the run, so some topics remain unverified (see Gaps). CC BY-SA files require attribution (author + license + link) AND share-alike of derivatives (if the site modifies/recolours an SVG, the modified file must be CC BY-SA). GPL/GFDL files are awkward for a web page (GFDL requires including the license text) — prefer the PD/CC alternatives.

### Gaps

## 2. NASA and FAA public-domain resources

### Takeaway
NASA Glenn's Beginner's Guide to Aeronautics and FAA PHAK figures are US-government works, generally reusable for education with credit, except NASA insignia and third-party credited images; NASA's classic simulators (FoilSim, EngineSim, Atmosphere applet) are Java applets, i.e. not runnable in modern browsers — link-only/legacy.

### Cited Findings
- NASA: media "generally are not subject to copyright in the United States"; "You may use this material for educational or informational purposes"; insignia/logotype use is "protected by law"; third-party copyrighted images are labelled with the rights holder and need the holder's permission — [NASA Images and Media guidelines](https://nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media)
- Beginner's Guide page "Aircraft Rotations" (yaw/pitch/roll axes, control surfaces, Wright 1902 glider), updated 21 Jul 2022; page itself carries no copyright statement — [NASA GRC Beginner's Guide](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/aircraft-rotations); related page [rotation control axes](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/rotation-control-axes)
- NASA GRC free software page lists FoilSim III (lift/drag of airfoils with stall model), EngineSim (jet engine design/test), TunnelSim, TunnelSys, RangeGames (multiple-choice aircraft performance questions), KiteModeler, Wright 1901 Wind Tunnel, Atmosphere Applet (pressure/temperature/density vs altitude, speed of sound, Mach); "every program is described as a Java applet", downloadable; page last updated 13 May 2021, no mention of Java deprecation — [NASA GRC Free Software](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/freesoftware_page.htm)
- FAA Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge FAA-H-8083-25C, page updated 3 Nov 2023, PDF per chapter: ch.4 Principles of Flight, 5 Aerodynamics of Flight, 6 Flight Controls, 7 Aircraft Systems, 8 Flight Instruments, 10 Weight and Balance, 12 Weather Theory, 13 Aviation Weather Services, 15 Airspace, 16 Navigation, 17 Aeromedical Factors — [FAA PHAK](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/phak). No explicit copyright statement on the page itself.
- Commons hosts PD derivatives of FAA PHAK figures (e.g. `Boeing 727 flight control surfaces`), and a category exists: [Category:Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (mirror)](https://download.osmand.net/wiki/Category:Pilot%27s_Handbook_of_Aeronautical_Knowledge)

### Inferences
- PHAK figures (US federal works, 17 U.S.C. §105) can be extracted as PNG for the site with "Source: FAA PHAK FAA-H-8083-25C" credit; check each figure for a third-party credit line before reuse. Figures are in English/US units (knots/feet fine for BIA, but US airspace classes differ from France — do NOT reuse PHAK ch.15 airspace figures for French airspace).
- NASA Java simulators: treat as link-only references; to offer interactivity, rebuild small JS widgets (airfoil lift vs angle, ISA calculator) inspired by them. NASA's newer JavaScript ports were not verified.

### Gaps
- Did not confirm whether NASA GRC published HTML5/JS versions of FoilSim/EngineSim (not stated on the page fetched).
- FAA "Web Policies and Notices" text not retrieved; public-domain status inferred from US federal-work rule, not from an FAA statement.

## 3. French official / open resources (SIA, Météo-France, DGAC, CIRAS, Éduscol)

### Takeaway
SIA aeronautical information is published under a French "Licence Ouverte" allowing free reuse (including commercial) with attribution (SIA + update date) and without distorting the data; this makes VAC excerpts usable as illustrations if credited and clearly marked "not for navigation". CIRAS/Éduscol content carries no explicit open licence — link-only.

### Cited Findings
- SIA "Licence Ouverte" (no version number): free, worldwide, non-exclusive, commercial and non-commercial reuse; may copy, distribute, adapt, extract, transform; must credit SIA as source and the date of last update (hyperlink acceptable); must not alter meaning or imply official endorsement; data provided as is, user responsible for flight-safety implications — [SIA licenceopendata-vf_2.pdf](https://www.sia.aviation-civile.gouv.fr/pub/media/news/file//l/i/licenceopendata-vf_2.pdf)
- SIA "produits numériques en libre disposition" are 0,00 € downloads from a customer account and reference that licence (e.g. "Complément aux cartes aéronautiques 2026") — [SIA product page](https://www.sia.aviation-civile.gouv.fr/produits-numeriques-en-libre-disposition/recueils/complement-aux-cartes-aeronautiques-2026-format-pdf-1ere-edition.html)
- From 2026 the SIA takes over the 1:500 000 OACI charts from IGN (IGN won't print them in 2026); same specifications; 2026 editions expected around mid-April; distribution via SIA's usual distributors; no mention of free digital availability — [Aerovfr, Mar 2026](https://www.aerovfr.com/2026/03/le-sia-reprend-les-ign-oaci/)
- SIA cartographic legend reference: "Recueil de critères cartographiques V.2.2 (01/11/2022)" — [SIA PDF](https://www.sia.aviation-civile.gouv.fr/media/reglementation/file/r/e/recueil_de_crit_res_cartographiques_v.2.2.pdf)
- open flightmaps: data under the "OFMA General Users License" (full text not on FAQ); France "LF" listed in downloads; "not every region is equally advanced"; data must not be used as primary navigation source — [open flightmaps FAQ](https://openflightmaps.org/faq/)
- Éduscol "Manuel BIA 2021" PDF (full and per-chapter, e.g. ch.2, ch.4) — [Manuel BIA 2021](https://eduscol.education.fr/sti/sites/eduscol.education.fr.sti/files/ressources/pedagogiques/11282/11282-bia-2021-vf1-copie.pdf), [ch.4](https://sti.eduscol.education.fr/sites/eduscol.education.fr.sti/files/ressources/pedagogiques/11284/11284-manuel-bia-2021-ch4.pdf); no licence stated on the CIRAS page linking it — [CIRAS Bordeaux](https://ent2d.ac-bordeaux.fr/disciplines/ciras/2022/04/02/ressources-de-preparation-au-bia/)
- Official past papers (sujets BIA) hosted on Éduscol STI, e.g. [sujet 2020](https://sti.eduscol.education.fr/sites/eduscol.education.fr.sti/files/concours-examens/12740/12740-sujet-bia-session-2020-copie.pdf)
- CIRAS Bordeaux also links a BIA chatbot (edcafe.ai) and pages on "logiciels libres pédagogiques" and "ressources numériques" — [CIRAS Bordeaux](https://ent2d.ac-bordeaux.fr/disciplines/ciras/2022/04/02/ressources-de-preparation-au-bia/). A post titled "arrêt du site" dated 28 May 2026 appears in search results — [CIRAS Bordeaux](https://ent2d.ac-bordeaux.fr/disciplines/ciras/2026/05/28/arret-du-site/) (content not read; links may break).

### Inferences
- VAC/OACI excerpts: SIA-published material can be shown with "Source : SIA, édition du JJ/MM/AAAA — ne pas utiliser pour la navigation". The 2026+ OACI 1:500 000 now being SIA products may fall under the same licence, but this is not confirmed for paper/IGN-heritage charts (IGN base map layers may carry separate rights) — treat OACI chart scans as link-only until confirmed.
- Éduscol manual and CIRAS courses: no explicit open licence found → link to PDFs, do not copy figures.

### Gaps
- Météo-France Aeroweb (aviation.meteo.fr) TEMSI/WINTEM sample reuse terms: not researched (access requires account; Météo-France open data under Etalab licence exists for some products, unverified for TEMSI). Treat as link-only.
- DGAC posters (sécurité des vols, espaces aériens): not researched.
- Exact licence of eAIP VAC PDFs specifically (vs. "produits en libre disposition") not explicitly confirmed on a VAC page.

## 4. Existing interactive tools (quizzes, apps, simulators, calculators)

### Takeaway
Several free French BIA quiz sites built on official annales exist (France-BIA is the most complete and requires no account); browser flight sim GeoFS is free; utility calculators (METAR, E6B, weight & balance, SkyVector) were only partially verified.

### Cited Findings
- **France-BIA quiz** — free, no sign-up/account/email, progress stored in browser; annales 2015–2026 (page states both "1 824" and "1 500" questions — inconsistent); 6 subjects; zones Europe-France, Asie-Pacifique, Hémisphère Sud; full 120-question paper, 20-question per-subject or random; training mode with explanations and exam mode graded /20; paid live courses separate; run by Sandra Bouxirot (CAEA pilot-instructor) — [france-bia.com](https://www.france-bia.com/quiz-annales-bia-gratuit); per-module pages e.g. [aérodynamique](https://www.france-bia.com/2025-entrainement-bia-aerodynamique-principes-vol), [histoire](https://www.france-bia.com/2025-entrainement-bia-histoire-aeronautique-spatial), [anglais](https://www.france-bia.com/2025-entrainement-bia-anglais-aeronautique), [random](https://www.france-bia.com/qcm-bia-aleatoire)
- **Annales du B.I.A.** — full course + QCM by year and theme (e.g. histoire 2021) — [annales-bia.fr/cours.php](https://annales-bia.fr/cours.php), [QCM 2021 histoire](https://annales-bia.fr/qcm.php?annee=2021&theme=his) (seen in search results; free status not checked)
- **Horizon BIA** — "QCM et Quiz BIA gratuits — entraînement par module" — [horizonbia.com](https://www.horizonbia.com/qcm-bia/) (search title only)
- **bia-gratuit.fr** — "Brevet Initiation Aéronautique 2026" — [bia-gratuit.fr](https://www.bia-gratuit.fr/) (search title only)
- **Travail-Industrie quiz** — "QCM BIA 2026 : 40 questions corrigées", 4 series — [travail-industrie.com](https://travail-industrie.com/nos-formations-en-ligne/quizz/qcm-bia-brevet-initiation-aeronautique) (search title only; generic training site, lower specialisation)
- **CIRAS chatbot** (edcafe.ai) — [link via CIRAS Bordeaux](https://ent2d.ac-bordeaux.fr/disciplines/ciras/2022/04/02/ressources-de-preparation-au-bia/)
- **GeoFS** — free flight simulator running in the browser — [korben.info article](https://korben.info/geofs-simulateur-vol-navigateur.html), [Clubic](https://www.clubic.com/telecharger-fiche624853-geofs.html); site geo-fs.com
- **E6B apps (iOS)**: E6BX E6B Flight Computer — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/id1462588103); E6b Pilot — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/e6b-pilot/id368495864) (price not checked)
- **NASA RangeGames / Atmosphere applet** — Java, legacy — [NASA GRC](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/freesoftware_page.htm)
- **open flightmaps** VFR charts (incl. LF France, variable completeness) — [openflightmaps.org](https://www.openflightmaps.org)

### Inferences
- For a teen, France-BIA (no account, explanations, exam mode) is the best external quiz to link; the custom site should focus on what these lack: visual explanations and interactive widgets (wind triangle, ISA calculator, METAR decoding exercises).
- Airspace viewing: link to SIA eAIP / VAC and open flightmaps rather than embedding tiles (OFMA licence not read in full).

### Gaps
- Not verified: METAR decoders in French (e.g. Aeroweb, metar-taf.com), online E6B/wind-triangle web calculators, SkyVector (US-centric; France coverage via world VFR not checked), SIA airspace viewer, weight & balance web tools, Android/iOS BIA apps (an App Store listing id979045912 appeared in search, content not checked: [apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id979045912)), "Aérobia", "BIA Facile", "QCM BIA" app names from the brief — none confirmed.
