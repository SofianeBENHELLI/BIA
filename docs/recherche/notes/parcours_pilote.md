# Parcours pilote en France après le BIA (état novembre 2026)

Research notes for a "Devenir pilote" section. Research done 22 Nov 2026. Several official pages (FFA, Air France, copeps.fr) could not be fetched directly; their figures come from secondary sources (académies' CIRAS pages, Air Journal) and are flagged.

## 1. Licences de loisir : ULM, LAPL(A), PPL(A), planeur (SPL)

### Takeaway
PPL(A): first solo at 16, licence at 17, 45 h minimum, 9 theory subjects with 75 % needed in each, class 2 medical, about 8 000–15 000 € in 2026. LAPL(A) is the lighter option: 30 h and about 6 000–12 000 €. In France the ULM theory exam has 60 questions and a 75 % pass mark. Gliding is the cheapest way in and has the strongest youth aids, but its minimum ages and costs were not confirmed from a primary source.

### Cited Findings
**PPL(A) / LAPL(A) (EASA Part-FCL, as applied in France)**
- PPL: licence at 17, first solo possible from 16. LAPL has the same ages. — [devispermis.fr PPL guide](https://devispermis.fr/blog/licence-ppl-avion-pilote-prive-guide)
- PPL minimum hours: 45 h total, made of 25 h dual and 10 h supervised solo. It also needs a solo cross-country of at least 270 km with 2 full-stop landings at other aerodromes. — [devispermis.fr](https://devispermis.fr/blog/licence-ppl-avion-pilote-prive-guide)
- LAPL: 30 h (15 h dual, 6 h solo) and a solo cross-country of at least 150 km. It covers single-engine piston aircraft up to 2 000 kg with at most 4 people on board. A LAPL can be upgraded to a PPL with 15 more hours, 10 of them dual, at a DTO/ATO (the article warns these conditions may change). — [devispermis.fr](https://devispermis.fr/blog/licence-ppl-avion-pilote-prive-guide)
- The theory exam is the same for LAPL and PPL, so a LAPL holder gets full theory credit toward the PPL. It has 9 subjects: air law, aircraft general knowledge, flight performance, human performance, meteorology, navigation, operational procedures, principles of flight and communications. — [devispermis.fr](https://devispermis.fr/blog/licence-ppl-avion-pilote-prive-guide)
- DGAC, official: the private exams (Avion/Hélicoptère) have **128 questions over 9 subjects**, with a **75 % pass mark per subject**. OCEANE exam centres take PPL and LAPL candidates. **The FFA now runs the Avion exams.** FFA-affiliated training organisations register candidates through "SMILE Examens", and non-affiliated ones use an online form sent to the FFA. — [ecologie.gouv.fr, Examens théoriques PN](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/examens-theoriques-devenir-personnel-navigant)
- The 2026 calendar of non-professional OCEANE exams is published by the DGAC. — [Calendrier OCEANE 2026 (PDF)](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/documents/Calendrier_examens_theoriques_non_professionnels_OCEANE_2026.pdf)
- Medical: PPL needs a class 2 certificate from an aviation medical examiner. It lasts 5 years under 40, 2 years from 40 to 50, and 1 year over 50. LAPL uses its own lighter medical certificate. — [devispermis.fr](https://devispermis.fr/blog/licence-ppl-avion-pilote-prive-guide)
- Indicative 2026 costs: **PPL 8 000–15 000 €**, **LAPL 6 000–12 000 €**. Theory preparation is billed separately and its price is not regulated. — [devispermis.fr](https://devispermis.fr/blog/licence-ppl-avion-pilote-prive-guide) (commercial comparison site, not an official source)

**ULM**
- DGAC ULM theory exam: 60 multiple-choice questions in 1 h 30. The pass mark is 75 % for ULM and 90 % for the I-ULM instructor exam. A pass certificate is valid 24 months. Exams are offered in both FFPLUM and DGAC centres during a 6-month transition period. — [ecologie.gouv.fr, Pilotes d'ULM](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/pilotes-dulm)
- There is a theory equivalence clause (§2.3 of the arrêté of 4 May 2000, amended 2006). The page does not say whether it covers the BIA. — [ecologie.gouv.fr](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/pilotes-dulm)

**Gliding**
- The FFVP signed a 2026-2029 agreement with the sports ministry, so the federation is active for that period. — [sports.gouv.fr PDF](https://www.sports.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2026-06/ff-vol-en-planeur-2026-2029-signe-6680.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a 15-year-old, gliding or ULM is the realistic first step. PPL/LAPL can only start solo at 16 and the licence comes at 17.
- Holding a LAPL first and upgrading later is a cheaper staged path, since the theory exam is shared.
- The FFA now runs the PPL/LAPL Avion theory exams, so aéroclubs register students through FFA's SMILE system. Worth saying on the site, because older guides only mention DGAC OCEANE.

### Gaps
- ULM minimum ages (commonly said to be solo at 15 and licence at 15 or 16) and typical ULM training cost: no primary source fetched. Check the arrêté of 31 July 1981 §4.5 or ffplum.fr.
- SPL (glider, Part-SFCL) minimum ages: commonly cited as solo at 14 and licence at 16, from training knowledge, NOT verified. Check EASA Part-SFCL.
- Typical glider training cost in a club: not found.
- The theory exam time and the exact number of questions per subject (only the 128-question total is confirmed). Whether the BIA gives any credit toward the PPL theory exam: not found (generally it does not, but unverified).
- The EASA Part-FCL primary text was not fetched. The age and hour figures come from a secondary source and match the commonly known Part-FCL figures (FCL.200 / FCL.210).

## 2. Aides et bourses

### Takeaway
All three federations run youth aids, and holding the BIA usually raises the amount. The figures below come from rectorat CIRAS pages dated May 2025, so **check them against the federations' own pages for 2026**.

### Cited Findings
- **FFA "Objectif pilote"**: financial help paid as the student reaches milestones toward the PPL, for people up to **21**. You apply through one of roughly 600 aéroclubs. The page gives no amount. — [CIRAS Nancy-Metz, 26/05/2025](https://sites.ac-nancy-metz.fr/ciras/des-aides-pour-preparer-le-brevet-de-pilote-prive-avion/)
- FFA bonuses of **up to 2 175 €** for under-21s training as pilots. — [CIRAS Bordeaux, "Voler après le BIA"](https://ent2d.ac-bordeaux.fr/disciplines/ciras/preparer-le-bia/3-voler-apres-le-bia/) (undated; check the amount on ffa-aero.fr)
- **FFVP (gliding)**: up to **1 500 €** in aid during training, up to age **25**. There are two kinds: objective-based bursaries ("bourses sur objectif") and special aids for deserving young pilots. **Some aids are increased for BIA holders.** Details at ffvp.fr/les-bourses. — [CIRAS Nancy-Metz, 26/05/2025](https://sites.ac-nancy-metz.fr/ciras/des-aides-financieres-pour-devenir-pilote-de-planeur/)
- **FFPLUM (ULM)**: **550 €** in total (400 € base + **150 € for BIA holders**), up to age **25**. You must be an FFPLUM member and train at an affiliated school. Details at ffplum.fr/pratiquer/subvention/aides-jeunes. — [CIRAS Nancy-Metz, 26/05/2025](https://sites.ac-nancy-metz.fr/ciras/?p=4743); older FFPLUM form: [AideJeunePilote2020](https://ffplum.fr/images/jeunes/AideJeunePilote2020-1.pdf)
- FFA and FFVP co-hosted the Air France Cadets information webinar (23 June 2026), which shows the federations are active in youth guidance. — [Air Journal, 17/06/2026](https://www.air-journal.fr/2026-06-17-air-france-rouvre-sa-filiere-cadets-une-formation-de-pilote-de-ligne-100-financee-5275748.html)

### Inferences
- In practice the BIA brings an extra bonus in the FFPLUM aid (+150 €) and in some FFVP aids, and it serves as a youth credential for aéroclubs.

### Gaps
- The exact 2025-2026 FFA amounts, how they are split by milestone, and whether there is a BIA bonus. ffa-aero.fr was not reached; the 2 175 € figure is unconfirmed.
- Aéro-Club de France / Fondation bourses, regional aids (Régions, departments), aéroclub youth rates, and the "Brevet de base" (understood to be discontinued under EASA rules; not verified): no sources found.
- The CAEA (instructor certificate for teaching the BIA) was not researched; it appears only in the title of an older FFPLUM form ([Dossier BIA-CAEA 2018](https://ffplum.fr/images/formulaires/Dossier%20BIA-CAEA%2009-2018.pdf)).

## 3. Programmes jeunesse et établissements

### Takeaway
The École des pupilles de l'air et de l'espace (Grenoble-Montbonnot) is the Air Force's own secondary school and recruits once a year in January–February, with admission priorities. Cadets de l'Air and EAJ details were not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- EPAE: applications for the 2026/2027 year (collège and lycée) were open from **7 January to 8 February 2026**, online through demarches-simplifiees.fr. — [defense.gouv.fr/epae, 07/01/2026](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/epae/actualites/ouverture-phase-candidature-0)
- EPAE priority groups: pupilles de la Nation and orphans of military or aviation families, children of serving or retired military, children of reservists with 10+ years of service, orphans of civil aviation, children of Ministry of the Armed Forces staff, civil servants and judges, and, for the lycée only, holders of national-education bourses. About 750 pupils, about 80 % boarders. — [defense.gouv.fr/epae](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/epae/actualites/ouverture-phase-candidature-0)
- The EPAE also runs CPGE classes, with its own admission circular. — [CIO Brest, circulaire CPGE EPA](https://www.cio-brest.ac-rennes.fr/sites/cio-brest.ac-rennes.fr/IMG/pdf/ecole_pupilles_de_l_air_circulaire_admission_classes_preparatoires_aux_grandes_ecoles.pdf); [Onisep EPAE](https://www.onisep.fr/ressources/structures-enseignement/auvergne-rhone-alpes/isere/ecole-des-pupilles-de-l-air-et-de-l-espace)
- The EETAAE (École d'enseignement technique de l'Air et de l'Espace, Saintes) recruits in première and terminale for 2026. This is a technical, non-pilot route. — [EETAAE avis première 2026](https://eetaa722.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/EETAAE-Avis-de-recrutement-2026-en-classe-de-premiere.pdf)
- Overview of the defence lycées: [defense.gouv.fr, Lycées militaires](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/sga/au-service-nation-du-public/jeunesse/vous-avez-entre-13-18-ans/lycees-militaires-defense)

### Gaps
- Cadets de l'Air (AAE) and Escadrilles Air Jeunesse: no current source fetched. Their age, cost and flying content in 2026 are unknown.
- A list of lycées with aeronautical sections or options: only an anecdotal press item (Lycée Henri-Cornat, Valognes, BIA option open to all pupils, Jan 2026: [ac-normandie press PDF](https://media.ac-normandie.fr/-/communication/revue-presse/2026/01/20/LPM_50_ok_Valognes_Option%20ouverte%20%e0%20tous%20les%20lyc%e9ens%20d'Henri-Cornat.%20Les%20brevets%20d'initiation%20%e0%20l'a%e9ronautique%20ont%20%e9t%e9%20remis.pdf)).

## 4. Pilote professionnel : ENAC EPL, cadets compagnie, ATPL

### Takeaway
ENAC EPL/S is almost free (1 500 €/year in fees) but very selective: 14 places for 1 537 candidates in 2025. It requires 60 ECTS in science (one year of science prépa or university), age 16 to under 23, and class 1 medical. Air France Cadets reopened in 2026, fully funded, and asks for a science bac+2. Self-funded ATPL costs about 60 000–100 000 €.

### Cited Findings
**ENAC EPL/S 2026** — [Notice EPL/S 2026 (PDF)](https://concours.enac.fr/concours/c13608/N_fr_NOTICE_EPLS_2026.pdf)
- Eligibility: EU/EEA nationality; **over 16 and under 23 on 1 January of the competition year**; 60 ECTS in a science curriculum (scientific CPGE, science licence or equivalent); census and JDC done; class 1 fitness; at most 3 attempts in total across all EPL tracks.
- About 14 places. In 2025: 1 537 registered, 131 passed the written stage, 14 admitted.
- Stages, all eliminatory:
  - Written: maths at PCSI level, physics at MPSI level, 2 h each, eliminated below 5/20; English, eliminated below 10/20.
  - One day of computer-based psychotechnical and psychomotor tests in Toulouse.
  - Psychological stage: group exercises and individual interviews with a psychologist and a pilot.
  - English oral: news-audio summary and conversation, eliminated below 10/20.
  - Class 1 medical at the CEMPN in Toulouse (July).
- 2026 calendar: registration 1 Dec 2025 – 30 Jan 2026; written exams 31 March 2026; psychotechnical tests 18–22 May; psychological tests and English oral 15–19 June; medical in July.
- Registration fee 190 €, free for French government bourse holders. Tuition **1 500 €/year**; ENAC pays all technical training costs. The student pays for housing and food (Biscarrosse, Carcassonne, Grenoble, Saint-Yan, Toulouse). A means-tested maintenance grant exists. Integrated ATPL course lasts about 24 months.
- Other ENAC tracks: EPL/U (for licence holders) — [Notice EPL/U 2026](https://concours.enac.fr/concours/c13611/N_fr_NOTICE_EPLUP_2026.pdf); CPATPL — [Notice CPATPL 2026](https://concours.enac.fr/concours/c13606/N_fr_NOTICE_CPATPL_2026.pdf)

**Air France Cadets 2026** — [Air Journal, 17/06/2026](https://www.air-journal.fr/2026-06-17-air-france-rouvre-sa-filiere-cadets-une-formation-de-pilote-de-ligne-100-financee-5275748.html)
- Applications ran from 15 June to 31 July 2026 ("Pilote Cadet Air France F/H").
- Training lasts about 24 months: about 9 months of ATPL theory, then 15–21 months of CPL, IR/ME and MCC, about 150 h of flight and 80 h of simulator. Graduates become first officers at Air France (A220/A320) or Transavia (737/A320neo).
- Cost: training fully funded by the airline.
- Previous conditions: scientific bac+2 or equivalent (CPGE, 120 ECTS), EEA or Swiss nationality, French and English, class 1 medical. **No formal age limit.** No prior flying needed.
- Selection: online pre-selection, psychotechnical and psychomotor tests (PSY1/PSY2), and individual and group interviews.
- 25 % of the 2025 cadets were women; more than 300 cadets since the 2018 relaunch.
- Self-funded training at ENAC or private schools: 60 000–100 000 €.
- Official page (not fetchable): [corporate.airfrance.com](https://corporate.airfrance.com/fr/actualites/devenez-pilote-la-campagne-de-recrutement-du-programme-cadets-air-france-est-ouverte)

### Inferences
- For a lycéen, the most realistic civil plan has two steps: a science bac with maths and physics-chemistry, then one or two years of science CPGE or licence. That makes them eligible for EPL/S (60 ECTS, under 23) and, with 120 ECTS, for the Air France Cadets.
- With 14 places for about 1 500 candidates, the site should describe EPL as an excellent option but not the only plan.

### Gaps
- Integrated vs modular ATPL cost split in 2026 (only the overall 60–100 k€ range found). Other airline cadet schemes (Transavia, Corsair, Air Austral, easyJet/CAE): not researched.
- Class 1 medical, common disqualifiers (vision limits, colour vision, refractive surgery rules): no source fetched. Check EASA Part-MED MED.B.070 and the CEMPN. Advice to do a class 1 check before investing heavily (for example in terminale) is common but unsourced here.

## 5. Pilote militaire

### Takeaway
The main 2026 routes are: (a) contract flying officer (OSC PN) in the Armée de l'Air et de l'Espace, under 27, French nationality; (b) the École de l'air et de l'espace competitive exams after CPGE (career officer); (c) Marine aéronavale, as contract officer after the bac or as career officer through the École navale; (d) ALAT, not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- AAE contract officer, flying (OSC PN): fighter, helicopter, transport or remote pilot, or weapons-systems navigator (NOSA). **Age under 27**, French nationality. Selection: psychotechnical tests, English test, sports tests, medical and motivation interview. Initial officer training lasts 12 weeks at the EFCC (4 weeks FMI + 8 weeks FMGO). — [EAE triptyque carrière courte (PDF)](https://ecole-air-espace.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/triptyque-devenir-aviateur-carriere-courte-EAE-avec-marge-blanche-100.pdf)
- EAE external exams for 2026: science CPGE (MP, MPI, PC, PSI) — [notice CE EAE CPGE 2026](https://www.ecole-air-espace.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/20251204_NP_DRHAAE-SDEF__notice-ce-eae-cpge-mp-mpi-pc-psi-2026.pdf); entry on qualifications — [notice titres 2026](https://www.ecole-air-espace.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/20251002_NP_DRHAAE_SDEF_BAF_DESC__notice_ce_eae_titres_2026.pdf); sciences — [notice sciences 2026](https://www.ecole-air-espace.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/20251204_NP_DRHAAE-SDEF_notice_sciences_2026.pdf)
- Candidate guide (recruitment centres, CIRFA): [devenir-aviateur.gouv.fr Guide du candidat](https://devenir-aviateur.gouv.fr/media/2264/download/GUIDE_DU_CANDIDAT%202.pdf)
- Marine aéronavale (EOPAN specialty, École navale, Brest):
  - Contract-officer route after the bac: 2 months of military and maritime training, then 26–45 months of EOPAN training.
  - Career route: science prépa, then 3 years at the École navale, then the same EOPAN training.
  - Aircraft: fighters, maritime patrol, Hawkeye and helicopters. Excellent vision and fitness required. — [Onisep, Pilote de l'aéronautique navale (PDF)](https://www.onisep.fr/ressources/univers-metier/metiers/pilote-de-l-aeronautique-navale/pdf)
- Marine recruitment leaflets: [lamarinerecrute.fr triptyque pilote](https://www.lamarinerecrute.fr/sites/default/files/orientationkit/documents/2022_MSRM_PAO_TRIPTYQUE_PILOTE_C_202_BD.pdf) (2022, may be out of date)
- Helicopter (general): one route is bac + under 29 + tests to become OSC-P after specific training. Military training is funded in return for a commitment to serve a set number of years. — [Onisep, Pilote d'hélicoptère](https://www.onisep.fr/ressources/univers-metier/metiers/pilote-d-helicoptere) (does not say which service; ALAT is likely)

### Inferences
- With the bac and good fitness, a lycéen could aim at OSC PN (under 27) without a long degree. The CPGE route to the EAE leads to a career-officer path.

### Gaps
- Diploma required for OSC PN in 2026 (bac vs bac+2), contract length, and the role of the CEMPN in military selection: not confirmed by the fetched page.
- ALAT (army helicopters, EMIA, sous-officier pilote, Dax/Le Luc schools): no primary source fetched.
- Age limits for the EAE CPGE exam: in the 2026 notices linked above, not extracted.

## 6. Calendrier recommandé pour un lycéen de 15-17 ans

### Takeaway
Synthesis from the findings above (no single official source):

| Age / class | Steps | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 15 (seconde) | BIA; first glider or ULM lessons (FFVP aid up to 1 500 €, FFPLUM aid 400 + 150 € with BIA, both up to 25); choose maths + physics-chemistry specialities; English | §1-2 |
| Jan–Feb of seconde/première | EPAE application if eligible (applications 7 Jan – 8 Feb in 2026) | §3 |
| 16 | First solo in aeroplane possible; start LAPL/PPL training, apply for FFA "Objectif pilote" (up to 21) | §1-2 |
| 17 (terminale) | LAPL/PPL licence possible; class 2 medical (and consider an early class 1 check, unsourced); Parcoursup: science CPGE / licence | §1, §4 |
| 18-19 | 1 year of science CPGE/licence = 60 ECTS gives EPL/S eligibility (registration Dec–Jan, written exam in March); after bac, apply to the Marine OSC route | §4-5 |
| 19-20 | 120 ECTS opens Air France Cadets (applications June–July in 2026); EAE CPGE exams | §4-5 |
| up to 22 / 26 | EPL/S last year (under 23 on 1 Jan); OSC PN AAE (under 27) | §4-5 |

### Gaps
- No official recommended timeline exists in the sources found. The table above is an inference.
